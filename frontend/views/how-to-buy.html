How to buy

From first idea to your own A-frame

Building a house can feel overwhelming. Here is the whole journey in seven clear steps – what you do, what we do, and what happens next.

  • The seven steps
  • ROH BAU or Turnkey

  1. Choose a model

    Compare the four models by size, rooms and price, and think about how you want to live – a compact retreat or a family home.

    You
    Browse the models and shortlist your favourites.
    We
    Answer any first questions by email or phone.
    Compare the models →

  2. Send an inquiry

    Tell us which model interests you, where your plot is and roughly when you would like to build.

    You
    Fill in the contact form – a few sentences are enough.
    We
    Get back to you to arrange a first conversation.
    Send an inquiry →

  3. Consultation and site visit

    We talk through your plans, visit the plot and check access, ground conditions and the best position for the house.

    You
    Share your wishes, budget and any documents you have for the plot.
    We
    Advise on the model, the package and any changes worth considering.

  4. Offer and contract

    You receive a written offer for your chosen model and package. When everything is clear, we sign the contract.

    You
    Review the offer and ask about anything that is unclear.
    We
    Prepare the offer, the contract and the schedule.

  5. Planning and permits

    The final drawings are prepared and the paperwork for the building permit goes to your municipality.

    You
    Sign the applications as the owner of the plot.
    We
    Prepare the drawings and help you with the documentation.

  6. Construction

    The foundation is prepared and your A-frame is built on site – the outside first, then, for turnkey projects, the interior.

    You
    Follow the progress – visits are welcome.
    We
    Build the house and keep you updated with photos.

  7. Handover

    We walk through the finished house together, go over every detail and hand you the keys.

    You
    Inspect the house and move in – or start your own interior if you chose ROH BAU.
    We
    Explain the house and stay available for questions afterwards.
