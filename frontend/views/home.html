About A-Frame Living

Built across Bosnia and Herzegovina

Welcome to A-Frame Living. Building a house is one of the most stressful projects in anyone's life, and guiding people through it is what we do – from the first sketch to the day they unlock the door.

That is why we work in the open. We show our real projects as they happened: the process, the challenges, the mistakes, the solutions and the results. No glossy promises – just honest work and A-frames that feel like home from the very first night.

We build four models – Atlantic, Norway, Scandinavia and Continental – and have completed projects from Velika Kladuša to Brčko, from Banja Luka to Jahorina.
